# Reed Coast: the next authored region

Aldenhold’s larger campaign is now revision 3, with **76×52 hexes (3,952 cells)**, exactly twice revision 2’s 52×38 area. The original 52×38 terrain and all old city, landmark and lair coordinates remain unchanged. Five new towns bring the world to twenty cities and strongholds. This is a playable Reed Coast increment toward the established eight-region, 128×80 design, not the completed six-act story.

The approach leaves Redford, passes through the Reed Coast Approach and reaches Willowford. The coastal river has two bridges, at Willow Crossing and Lantern Crossing. West-bank marshes and woodland contrast with east-bank farms, coastal hills and the sea. Brineport’s beacon and the Drowned Causeway are actual visitable sites. Coastal councils defend their own cities but do not launch rival expeditions into the player’s distant homeland; this prevents unseen extra invasions while the existing chapter is still being played.

## Regional and local stories

**R01 The Reed Coast Compact** opens after The Inheritance. It has no travel deadline. Meet the Reedhaven speaker, reach Willowford and fund crossing repairs, settle Miretooth’s tolls by negotiation or defeat its two troll enforcers at the Drowned Causeway, then sign at Brineport. The compact grants allied passage through all five towns and a one-time 200-gold contribution. The towns keep their populations, taxes and garrisons. The peaceful route costs 380 gold before the contribution; the military alternative replaces the 160-gold toll settlement with a battle. A defeat permits a later retry, without duplicating rewards.

**Q40 The Reed Census** is discovered near Reedspire. Reviewing records reveals 180 displaced residents. An 80-gold grant restores them to the town’s actual population and future workforce.

**Q44 The Swan Banner** is discovered near Gullmere. Visit the Brineport Beacon, copy its signal code and report to Gullmere for allied passage and a one-time 120-gold reward. It is an exploration story with no required battle.

Regional campaigns appear separately from the two relevant local highlights. Current-stage waypoints drive map navigation and local quest cards, including peaceful stops beside neutral garrisons. Realm-wide objectives remain a separate layer. The existing campaign victory conditions are preserved; these coastal stories do not claim to implement all later acts or postwar continuation.

## Save compatibility

Revision-2 imports add only the five towns, their economies and garrisons, and new locked quest records. Existing town populations and ownership, paid projects, goods, armies and IDs, treasury, random state, equipment, scouting and active battles remain unchanged. New IDs are allocated above the saved counter; added troops receive no mid-month movement. Revision-1 saves migrate through revision 2 first. Re-importing a current save adds nothing. Partial old registries and armies outside their historical bounds are rejected. The save schema and browser storage keys remain version 18 and unchanged through the game’s rename.

## Artwork and scope

The new city-building atlas contains distinct houses, barns, lumber workshops, mines, forges and treasuries. Stonework, quiet paving and small improvement labels support the richer textures. Repeated house labels and street clutter are omitted. Building cover, pillage, footprint limits and unit positions retain their gameplay rules. Existing city panoramas and map markers remain available; unique coastal portraits and additional local stories can be added later.
